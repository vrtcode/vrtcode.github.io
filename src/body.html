01 / About me

The docs say one thing. The code says, 'nah.'

That's the stuff I like digging into: Android and iOS integrations, native code, network protocols, and backend logic that doesn't quite match the story. I chase the cause, put the theory on trial, and turn what holds up into a repeatable check or a focused fix.

I also work on the less flashy bits that keep systems from falling over: queues, worker shutdowns, access checks, logs, and safe rollouts. A green build isn't a victory lap. Neither is one passing test. I keep 'we checked this' separate from 'that's our best guess.'
