01 / About

Where code, documentation, and behavior disagree.

I'm interested in systems where the code, documentation, and observed behavior don't quite agree. My work spans Android and iOS integrations, native code analysis, network protocols, and server-side logic. I connect research with engineering: investigate the cause, test a hypothesis, and turn the finding into a reproducible check or a focused change.

Reliability is another part of that work: worker lifecycles, task queues, access control, observability, and safe updates. A successful build, a passing local test, and verified behavior in an external environment are different kinds of evidence. I aim to make clear what has been established and what remains a hypothesis.
